## [0.1.1]

- Added an app icon, shown in Finder, Launchpad, the DMG and System Settings.

## [0.1.0]

First release.

- Capture an area, the previous area, the full screen or a window (with its shadow), with a timer, or capture text with OCR.
- Smart selection: drag for an area, click for the window under the cursor, click the desktop or press Enter for the full screen.
- Record an area, a window or the full screen to MP4 or GIF, with system audio, microphone, click highlighting and a countdown.
- Annotation editor with arrows, shapes, text, counters, highlighter, pixelate or blur, spotlight and crop, plus backgrounds with gradients or your wallpaper.
- Video editor to trim recordings, export at a lower resolution or frame rate and add the same backgrounds.
- Quick Access thumbnails, pinned screenshots, history, one global shortcut per action and auto-save. In English and Spanish.
